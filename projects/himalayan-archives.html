The Himalayan Archives

Turning Elizabeth Hawley's half-century of mountaineering documentation into a data story about a century of Himalayan expeditions, 1905–2024.

Dataset11,000+ expedition reports (Mavin Analytics)
Scale89,000+ mountaineers, 480 peaks
ToolsSQL Server, Excel, Power BI
RoleData Analyst

The Objective

The Himalayan Database, built on Elizabeth Hawley's decades of expedition documentation, holds over 11,000 expedition reports covering more than 89,000 mountaineers across 480 peaks. The goal was to turn that raw archive into clear, explorable trends about who has climbed, what's been climbed, and how the sport has changed since 1905.

Approach

I cleaned the data in SQL Server — standardizing dates, filtering invalid records, and consolidating inconsistent nationality data — then structured it into a star schema with fact and dimension tables before building the visualizations in Power BI.

About 23% of the 480 documented peaks remain unclimbed — potential first-ascent opportunities. Expeditions surged from the 1980s onward as equipment improved and commercial guiding took off, with sharp dips in 2015 (the earthquake-triggered avalanche) and 2020 (the pandemic). Everest's 327 recorded deaths alongside its 12,874 successful summits underline both the mountain's draw and its risk.
